4. DETAILED CROSS-STOCK DYNAMICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. STLTECH vs. HFCL: The Optical Pair Loop
   - STLTECH Leads: Driven by foreign institutional activity and direct global 
     export contracts. Multi-day volume expansion in STLTECH indicates institutional 
     accumulation.
   - HFCL Follows: Trades at a lower nominal unit price (sub-Rs 300 vs STLTECH's 
     higher price tag), making it the preferred high-beta vehicle for retail and 
     momentum traders. HFCL typically breaks out 24 to 48 hours after STLTECH.
   - The Top Signal: When STLTECH shows institutional distribution (high delivery 
     volume with stagnating price), HFCL usually tops out within 48 hours and 
     undergoes sharper percentage corrections.

B. SYRMA SGS vs. The Basket: The EMS Orbit
   - Syrma SGS does not track optical cables or defense hardware on an operational level.
   - Syrma SGS operates in the electronics manufacturing orbit alongside Dixon 
     Technologies and Kaynes Technology.
   - When Dixon/Kaynes rally on PCBA/semiconductor policy, Syrma acts as the value 
     catch-up trade. It links to HFCL primarily when broad telecom equipment PLI 
     announcements are released.

C. MTAR Technologies: Fundamental Independence
   - MTAR operates outside telecom networking. It drives revenue through clean 
     energy power units (Bloom Energy), aerospace components (ISRO), and nuclear 
     reactor sub-assemblies.
   - Market correlation to STLTECH/HFCL is thematic only: Algorithmic trading baskets 
     group MTAR under "AI Data Center Power & Ancillaries."
   - Synchronization occurs primarily during macro sell-offs due to MTAR's high 
     trailing multiple (~160x+).


5. DAILY MONITORING INDICATORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STLTECH Delivery % vs. HFCL Volume:
   If STLTECH delivery falls below 25% while HFCL traded volume doubles, the optical 
   trade is shifting from institutional accumulation to late-stage retail momentum.

2. Global Optical Comps (Corning Inc. / CIENA):
   Positive earnings beats or raised optical guide from Corning typically trigger an 
   opening gap in STLTECH, followed by sympathy buying in HFCL by mid-session.

3. Dixon Technologies Breakouts:
   Monitor Dixon's technical resistance breaks as a direct leading signal for 
   afternoon institutional bids in Syrma SGS.

4. Bloom Energy Backlog Updates:
   Bookings growth reported by Bloom Energy serves as the primary leading revenue 
   indicator for MTAR's precision fuel cell export segment.
