Demand (D-OB) & Supply (S-OB) Blocks

The origin down/up candle before an aggressive expansion marks where institutional algorithmic limit orders were filled. When price retraces to this unmitigated block, institutions defend their orders—giving you precise entry and tight stop loss in advance.

🎯 Rule: Enter on 1st retest of D-OB; invalidation strictly below block bottom.
⚡
Fair Value Gaps (3-Bar Imbalance)

Occurs when a violent candle leaves a gap between Bar 1 wick and Bar 3 wick. Algorithmic engines treat unfilled imbalances as price voids and rebalance back to the 50% midpoint (Consequent Encroachment) before continuation.

🎯 Rule: Unfilled FVGs act as high-probability magnet targets and elastic pullback zones.
🪤
Liquidity Sweeps (BSL & SSL Hunts)

Retail stops cluster above equal swing highs (Buy-Side Liquidity) and below equal lows (Sell-Side Liquidity). Market makers spike through these levels to trigger stop cascades, absorb counterparty volume, and reverse instantly.

🎯 Rule: Never chase breakout wicks outside range; wait for the sweep rejection candle to close.
📐
Session VWAP & ±1.5σ Dispersion

VWAP is the institutional fair value benchmark for the session. The ±1.5σ standard deviation bands quantify statistical overextension. Trend days ride the upper band; mean-reversion days bounce between upper and lower extremes.

🎯 Rule: Bullish bias while above VWAP; take profit / reverse at outer 1.5σ exhaustion band.
📊
Volume Profile POC & Value Area (VAH/VAL)

Point of Control (POC) is the single price level with the highest traded volume. The Value Area (70% of session volume between VAH and VAL) reveals where buyers and sellers agree on price. Breaks outside the Value Area trigger fast expansions.

🎯 Rule: POC acts as institutional gravity center; trade breakouts from VAL to VAH.
