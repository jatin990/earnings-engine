📐 Mathematical Calculation & Breakout Scoring Engine (0 – 100 Pts)

Breakout Radar scans the price sequence point-in-time and scores stocks based on volatility compression (coiling), volume expansion, resistance proximity, trend momentum, and fundamental earnings quality.

1. 🎯 Resistance Proximity (0–40 Pts)
Distance to 20-Day or 52-Week ceiling: (Price - High) / High × 100. Prices within 0% to -3% award 25–40 pts. Close proximity indicates higher breakout probability.
2. 📊 Volume Surge (0–10 Pts)
Volume expansion vs 20-Day moving average: Today Vol / 20D Avg Vol. Ratios of 1.0x to 1.5x award 10 pts; extreme spikes (>2.0x) are penalized to 4 pts to filter fakeout/churn.
3. 🗜️ Price Compression (0–10 Pts)
ATR(14) volatility squeeze: ATR(14) / Price. Low ATR% indicates coiling price energy ready for expansion.
4. 🚀 Trend Momentum (0–25 Pts)
Positive 30-day slope with price trading above the 20-Day SMA (Close > SMA20 & Ret30D > 0). Strong trend continuation yields up to 25 pts.
5. 📈 Fundamental Quality (0–15 Pts)
Normalized 3-Quarter EPS surprise streak (Q1/Q2/Q3 Quality Score 1-9 mapped to 0-15 pts).
6. 🎯 Target & Stop Loss Levels
Trigger = Resistance High; Target = Trigger + max(8%, 2×ATR); Stop Loss = Signal Price - min(5%, 1.5×ATR).
