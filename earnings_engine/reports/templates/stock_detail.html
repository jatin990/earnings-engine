Stock Intelligence & Calculation Architecture

                        
How data is ingested, AI consensus is synthesized, and quantitative parameters are computed

                    

                

                
            


            
            

                
                
                

                    

                        📥 Data Ingestion & Storage Pipeline
                    

                    

                        
• Price History: Daily OHLCV series ingested from NSE Bhavcopy & Yahoo Finance into SQLite price_history table (cached daily; live quotes refresh in real-time).

                        
• Earnings & EPS: Corporate quarterly financial results & EPS estimates stored in earnings_history table.

                        
• Institutional Holdings: Quarterly shareholding disclosures (FII/DII/Mutual Funds/HNIs) and bulk/block deals in institutional_activity.

                        
• Point-in-Time Separation: All indicators strictly filter price <= asof_date (T-1) to prevent future lookahead bias.

                    

                


                
                

                    

                        🧠 Multi-Model AI Consensus Engine
                    

                    

                        
• Ensemble Models: DeepSeek-R1 (28% wt - Chain-of-thought logic), Llama-3.3-70B (24% wt - Macro & quant), Qwen-2.5-72B (24% wt - Patterns & volume), Mistral-Large (24% wt - Catalysts & filings).

                        
• Consensus Target: Confidence-weighted median of individual model target prices adjusted for structural resistance.

                        
• Stop Loss Floor: Confidence-weighted invalidation price anchored to key support levels.

                        
• Agreement Strength %: [Sum(Agreeing Model Confidence) / Sum(Total Confidence)] * 100%.

                    

                


                
                

                    

                        🔮 Kronos AI 14-Day Deep Neural Sequence Forecast
                    

                    

                        Autoregressive deep sequence neural network (PyTorch / GPU) trained on multi-scale price action, volume profiles, and volatility regimes. Projects sequential 14-day forward closing prices [t+1 ... t+14] with dynamic support/resistance envelope channels.
                    

                


                
                

                    

                        📐 Core Quantitative Metrics & Formulas
                    

                    

                        

                            

                                EPS Surprise %
                                [(Actual EPS - Est EPS) / |Est EPS|] * 100%
                            

                            
Magnitude of quarterly earnings beat against consensus estimates.

                        


                        

                            

                                Deliverable Volume Spike
                                Today Delivery Vol / 20D Mean Delivery Vol
                            

                            
Flags institutional accumulation when ratio ≥ 2.0x.

                        


                        

                            

                                21-Day Annualized Volatility
                                StdDev(Daily Returns, 21D) * sqrt(252)
                            

                            
Measures annualized historical price dispersion for risk calibration.

                        


                        

                            

                                52-Week Range Position
                                (Close - 52W Low) / (52W High - 52W Low) * 100%
                            

                            
0% = At 52W Low, 100% = At 52W High (Breakout zone).
