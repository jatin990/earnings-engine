Options Intelligence & Quantitative Guide

Core Derivatives Architecture & Strike Matrix

The Options Hub aggregates live NSE F&O tick-by-tick feeds, open interest distributions, and analytical Greeks across all active index and equity contracts.

📊 Put-Call Ratio (PCR OI & Vol)
Ratio of total Put Open Interest to Call Open Interest. PCR > 1.2 indicates bullish institutional put writing (support), while PCR < 0.7 signals heavy call writing (resistance).
🎯 Max Pain Theory
The exact strike price where option buyers experience maximum cumulative financial loss at expiration, acting as an institutional pinning attractor.

SLIDE 2 OF 6

Dealer Gamma Exposure (GEX) Formulation

Market makers (dealers) who sell options to retail participants maintain a delta-neutral book. Their dynamic hedging creates massive structural flows in the underlying index:

Call GEX (₹ Lakhs) = (CE_OI × Gamma × Spot² × 0.01) / 100,000
Put GEX (₹ Lakhs) = (-PE_OI × Gamma × Spot² × 0.01) / 100,000
Net GEX (₹ Lakhs) = Call GEX + Put GEX
• Call GEX represents positive gamma. As price rises, dealers must sell futures/equities to hedge, suppressing rallies.
• Put GEX represents negative gamma. As price falls, dealers must sell into dropping markets, accelerating selloffs.
SLIDE 3 OF 6

Market Regimes: Positive vs. Negative Gamma

🟢 POSITIVE GAMMA REGIME

Dealers are long gamma. Hedging requires buying dips and selling rallies. Market exhibits low realized volatility, tight trading ranges, and strong mean-reversion around key strikes.

🔴 NEGATIVE GAMMA REGIME

Dealers are short gamma. Hedging forces selling into declines and buying into breakouts. This triggers volatility expansion, gamma squeezes, and explosive directional trends.

🔄 Gamma Flip Strike: The cumulative zero-crossing strike where dealer gamma transitions from positive to negative. Crossing below the Flip level signals an immediate regime change into high-volatility momentum.
SLIDE 4 OF 6

Analytical Black-Scholes Greeks & 0-DTE Guards

Our quantitative engine computes closed-form Black-Scholes Greeks for every individual strike, with numerical boundary guards for zero IV and 0-DTE expiry sessions:

Delta (Δ): Sensitivity of option price to underlying move ($\partial V / \partial S$). Call Δ ∈ [0, 1], Put Δ ∈ [-1, 0].
Gamma (Γ): Rate of change in delta per unit move in underlying ($\partial^2 V / \partial S^2$). Peaks at ATM strikes.
Theta (Θ): Time decay loss per day. Accelerates exponentially in the final 72 hours before expiry.
Vega (ν): Sensitivity of contract price to 1% change in Implied Volatility (IV).
🛡️ Boundary Protection: Enforces $\max(DTE/365, 10^{-6})$ and $\max(IV/100, 10^{-4})$ to prevent division by zero during intraday expiry hours.
SLIDE 5 OF 6

ATM Straddle Expected Move Bands (±1σ / ±2σ)

The market-implied pricing cone represents where institutional market makers price terminal index settlement based on ATM Straddle premiums:

ATM Straddle Price = CE_ATM_LTP + PE_ATM_LTP
1σ Expected Range (68.2% Conf) = Spot ± ATM_Straddle
2σ Tail Range (95.0% Conf) = Spot ± (1.85 × ATM_Straddle)

• Prices trading outside the 1σ band indicate aggressive institutional tail-hedging or high-conviction breakout momentum.
• Rejections at the 1σ/2σ bands frequently offer asymmetric mean-reversion counter-trend entries.
